Screener methodology: momentum (0–80) is an OR-blend of holder Δ (0–40, holder velocity & growth over ~5m) and volume Δ (0–40, 1m/5m volume vs the token's own 1h average, or vs the previous 5m), so one strong delta is enough to rank; + confirmation (−4–20: buy pressure, price, smart money) − penalties (holders draining, bot-heavy activity, near-gate supply concentration, youth). Hard gates block outright: rug ratio, wash trading, honeypot, liquidity, holder count, and supply control (top-10, bundled, insider, dev, sniper share; live mint/freeze authority). Fresh launches (still on the launchpad curve, or under an hour old) swap the liquidity floor for a market-cap floor, since a curve reserve or a minutes-old pool isn't DEX liquidity. Data: GMGN.ai OpenAPI.
